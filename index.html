About Joe Ferrandino

Personalized Service. Institutional Expertise.

Joe is an investment sales associate at Northeast Private Client Group®, covering the New York Hudson Valley and Western Connecticut markets. He provides strategic guidance to owners of multi-tenant retail, mixed-use, and industrial properties.

Before joining Northeast Private Client Group®, Joe held positions across key areas of the commercial real estate industry, including acquisitions, appraisals, lending, and property management. He earned his bachelor's degree in finance from Binghamton University School of Management in 2017.

In his role, Joe helps clients optimize their property value and make informed decisions when it comes time to sell or refinance. He guides sellers through a seamless disposition process and leverages an extensive network of local and regional buyers to generate a competitive bidding environment.

With deep roots in New York and Connecticut, Joe is committed to the long-term success of the clients and communities he serves.
